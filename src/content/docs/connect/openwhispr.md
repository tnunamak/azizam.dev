# Connect OpenWhispr

Use these settings to configure OpenWhispr as a Client for your gateway.

## Key setup

**Status:** documented
**Checked:** 2026-10-13

### Settings

- **Endpoint URL:** `https://<your gateway>/openai/v1`
- **API Key (Optional):** `<your key>`
- **Model:** `<model name>`
- **Endpoint URL:** `https://<your gateway>/openai/v1`
- **API Key (Optional):** `<your key>`
- **Model:** `<model name>`

### Steps

1. Open Settings, then Speech-to-Text. Under Cloud Providers, choose Custom and fill in the first group of fields.
2. Optional, to tidy up what you dictate: open Language Models, then Dictation Cleanup. Under Cloud Providers, choose Custom, fill in the second group, choose Apply & Refresh and pick the model.

> Note: Field names come from OpenWhispr's source code; menus can move between versions.
